# What goes into C:\drv on this board, staged by tools\mksysfs.py.

# Names here are the images in build\apps; they are copied in lower case,
# because that is what `drv install` writes and littlefs does not fold case.
#
# NODELN32.SYS, not NODELINK.SYS.  This board is an ESP32 - an LX6 - and the
# S3 it is wired to is an LX7.  A .SYS built for one does not refuse to load on
# the other: it loads and throws an illegal instruction somewhere inside, which
# reads as a driver bug rather than as the wrong image.  Two boards on one wire
# need two builds of the driver on that wire.
