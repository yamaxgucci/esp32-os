# What goes on this board's C:, staged by tools\mksysfs.py.
#
# The rule, the same as on the other two: a board carries the images its
# NEIGHBOURS can execute as well as its own.  This one is a LEAF with a single
# neighbour, the C3, so its own Xtensa LX7 and the C3's RISC-V are what it
# needs to hand anything over.
#
# THE CYD'S LX6 BUILDS ARE HERE ANYWAY, and that is deliberate: the CYD is two
# hops away, a program sent there passes through the C3, and the board that
# ASKS is the one that must carry an image the far board can run.  Three small
# files against a 4 MB flash is not a saving worth a journey that fails at the
# far end.
#
# Xtensa LX7, for this board itself:
NODEHI.AXE
MIGRANT.AXE
GRIND.AXE
# RISC-V, for the C3 next door:
NODEHIRV.AXE
MIGRANRV.AXE
GRINDRV.AXE
# Xtensa LX6, for the CYD two hops away:
NODEHI32.AXE
MIGRAN32.AXE
GRIND32.AXE
